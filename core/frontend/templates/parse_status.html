TRI-STATE, and the middle one is why this comment is longer than it was: parsed / failed / in-flight. On 2026-08-21 twenty JDs sat in exactly that state for a day. So the trigger is kept ONLY while a parse is genuinely in flight: not parsed AND not failed. `failure_reason` is what separates the two, and it has been on `JobOut` and arriving here unused the whole time. #}
